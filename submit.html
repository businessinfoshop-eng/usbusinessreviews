Reader Submissions

Submit a business for review.

Know of a small business that deserves editorial coverage? Tell us about it. Our team reviews every submission and selects businesses to research based on community interest and editorial merit.

What makes a good submission

We review small, independent businesses serving consumers in the United States. The best submissions include:

The business name and city. A short note about why this business stands out — exceptional service, unique offering, longstanding reputation, or a concerning pattern worth investigating. Any specific details that would help our research team get started: website, owner name, or distinctive products.

What we cannot do

We cannot guarantee that every submitted business will be reviewed. We receive far more submissions than we can cover, and our editorial team selects based on broader factors including consumer interest and regional importance.

We do not accept payment for review consideration, and we do not give priority to businesses that submit themselves over those submitted by readers.

Submit by email

Send your submission to tips@usbusinessreviews.org with the subject line "Business submission" and include the details listed above.

Our submissions form is coming soon — for now, email is the fastest way to reach our editorial team.

Note for Business Owners
If you own the business you are submitting, please disclose this in your message. We accept self-submissions but always note them as such during the research phase.
