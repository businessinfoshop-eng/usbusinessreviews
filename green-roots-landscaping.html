Green Roots Landscaping

A standout in the Austin metro market, distinguished by certified horticulturists, transparent project management and a consistent track record of on-time delivery on residential and commercial projects.

Reviewed by The Editorial Team Published May 14, 2026 Reading Time 5 min

Green Roots Landscaping has built a remarkable reputation across the Austin metro area since its founding in 2015. Specializing in residential and commercial landscaping, the company employs a team of certified horticulturists who consistently deliver projects on time and on budget. Their portfolio spans drought-resistant garden design to full irrigation system installations, making them a versatile choice for Texas homeowners navigating an increasingly challenging climate.

What truly separates Green Roots from competitors is their transparent project management process. Clients receive a detailed scope of work before any job begins, and the team maintains clear communication throughout. The company holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and has been recognized by Austin Monthly as a top home services provider for three consecutive years.

What stood out

Across dozens of project case studies and direct interviews, three qualities surfaced repeatedly. First, every Green Roots project is staffed by horticulturists holding professional certification — a rarity in residential landscaping. Second, pricing is presented upfront and in writing, with no ambiguity around materials versus labor. And third, the company's project managers communicate proactively rather than reactively, sending status updates without being prompted.

The result is a business that customers describe in remarkably consistent terms: reliable, professional, worth the wait. Texas climate has made landscaping uniquely difficult in recent years, and Green Roots has leaned into that challenge with native-plant expertise that competitors often lack.

Pros and considerations

Strengths
  • Certified horticulturists on every project
  • Transparent, written upfront pricing
  • Strong track record for timeliness
  • A+ Better Business Bureau rating
  • Deep native-plant expertise
Considerations
  • Waitlist runs 4–6 weeks in peak season
  • Premium pricing vs. local competitors
  • Limited service area outside Austin metro

The editorial verdict

Final Word
Green Roots Landscaping earns our highest recommendation for Austin homeowners who prioritize quality and reliability over rock-bottom pricing. Those who do engage Green Roots consistently report the investment is worthwhile.
