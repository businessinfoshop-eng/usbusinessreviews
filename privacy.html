Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. This policy describes how US Business Reviews collects and uses information.

Information we collect

We collect minimal information about visitors to our site. This includes anonymized analytics data such as page views, referring sources, and general geographic region. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily submit it through email or our forms.

How we use information

Anonymized analytics help us understand which content readers find useful so we can improve our editorial coverage. We do not sell or share visitor data with third parties.

Information you submit

If you contact us by email or submit a business for review, we retain that information for editorial purposes. We may follow up with you to clarify details or notify you if the business you submitted is covered.

Cookies

Our site uses minimal cookies for analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to read our reviews.

Third-party services

We use Vercel for hosting and basic analytics. We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or social media pixels on this site.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@usbusinessreviews.org.
