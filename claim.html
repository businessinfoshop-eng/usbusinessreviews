For Business Owners

Claim your business listing.

If your business has been reviewed by US Business Reviews, you can claim your listing to keep your business details accurate and respond to our editorial team.

What claiming gets you

Verified business owner status displayed on your review page. The ability to update contact information, hours, and other factual details. A direct line to our editorial team for corrections and updates. Notification when your annual review update is scheduled.

What claiming does not change

Claiming your listing does not affect your editorial review or score in any way. Our editorial team maintains complete independence over the content of our reviews.

How to claim

Email claims@usbusinessreviews.org from an email address associated with your business domain, with the subject "Claim listing — [Business Name]."

Include the URL of your business's review page and any documentation that confirms your ownership or authorization to represent the business.

Verification

Our team will verify your claim within five business days. Once verified, your listing will display a "Claimed by Owner" badge and you will have access to update factual details.
